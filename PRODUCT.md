# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Casual players on desktop or mobile who want a quick solo game.

## Product Purpose

A short number-guessing game: players try to find a secret number from 1 to 20 within three attempts.

## Positioning

The game combines a narrow number range, three lives, and higher/lower feedback for a quick, self-contained challenge.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Start a game, submit guesses, receive higher/lower hints, win or lose, and restart.
- The secret number is from 1 through 20; players have three valid guesses.
- Keep the existing game mechanics and features when changing the interface.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the arcade cabinet and established dark, lime, amber, and pink palette. Use a bold, playful game UI across the start screen and gameplay, with SVG heart icons for remaining lives.

## Product Principles

- Make the rules and remaining attempts easy to understand.
- Keep each game quick to start and play.
- Preserve clear feedback for guesses and game outcomes.
